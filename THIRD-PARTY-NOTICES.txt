Curskin Installer - Third-Party Notices
=======================================

Curskin Installer embeds the following unmodified helper executables in
compressed form inside CurskinInstaller.exe:

  avifdec.exe    libavif 1.4.2, Windows x64
  resvg.exe      resvg 0.47.0, Windows x64

They are extracted on first use to:

  %LOCALAPPDATA%\CurskinInstaller\tools\

and are used only to decode AVIF/AVIS cursor images and render SVG cursors.
They are not installed as system components.
